How I work

Whether it's a school brief or a client job, I follow the same steps.

  1. 1

    Listen

    I find out who it's for, where it will be used and what the result needs to achieve.

  2. 2

    Sketch

    I explore several directions at once. Quickly on paper first, then on the computer.

  3. 3

    Refine

    I polish the chosen design: type, colours, spacing. Then I work in your feedback.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    I prepare print-ready files, social media assets or launch the finished website.

I study IT at secondary school and spend every spare moment on graphic design. I love it when every detail has a reason.
